01

Look for a take-back program

Ask a local pharmacy, clinic, or municipal waste service whether they accept unused or expired medicines. This is often the preferred option.

02

Keep medicines secure

Keep items in their original packaging and out of reach of children and pets while arranging disposal. Do not share unused prescriptions.

03

Follow local instructions

Rules differ by location and medicine type. Check the package leaflet or ask a pharmacist before placing anything in household waste or flushing it.

This general guidance is not a substitute for local regulations. For a medicine-specific disposal question, contact a pharmacist or local waste authority.
